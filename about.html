About Us

Learn more about Kemo Citrus.

Our History

Kemo Citrus was established in 2020 as a family-focused citrus farming initiative during a difficult economic period.

The organisation aims to build a sustainable source of income while creating future opportunities for the family and community.

Our Mission

To produce quality citrus products while creating sustainable opportunities and contributing positively to the community.

Our Vision

To become a trusted citrus producer known for quality, reliability, sustainability and customer service.
