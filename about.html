About Us

Learn more about Kemo Citrus.

Our History

Kemo Citrus was established in 2020 after the founders recognised the challenges created by unemployment and the economic difficulties experienced during the COVID-19 period.

The organisation was created as a family-owned citrus farming business with the goal of creating a sustainable source of income and future employment opportunities for the family and the wider community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to produce high-quality citrus products while creating sustainable opportunities and contributing positively to our community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted South African citrus producer known for quality, reliability, sustainability and excellent customer service.

Our Values

  • Quality
  • Integrity
  • Hard work
  • Customer satisfaction
  • Sustainability
  • Community development
